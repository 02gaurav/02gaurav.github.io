Delete your TezMind account

TezMind by Gaurav Kumar

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open TezMind and tap your profile picture at the top of the home screen.
  2. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete, then confirm with the same Google account.

Without the app

Email 0202gaurav@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my TezMind account". We will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your TezMind sign-in account (Firebase user ID)
  • Your profile record: name, email address, profile photo link, sign-in method, and account created and last sign-in dates
  • Your daily challenge leaderboard results (name shown, time, mistakes, hints and score)
  • Your game history and settings on the device where you delete the account in the app

Nothing is kept after deletion. Game history on other devices stays there until you clear the app's data or uninstall it, because it is never uploaded. Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is handled under Google's Privacy Policy.
