Privacy policy

Effective 15 October 2026 · Applies to the TezMind app for Android and iOS

TezMind ("the app", "we") is a puzzle and brain-training game made by Gaurav Kumar, an individual developer. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

WhatWhyWhere it's kept
Your Google account name, email address, profile photo link and a Firebase user ID, when you sign in with Google To create your account, show your name and photo in the app, and keep you signed in Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (a users record with these fields, your sign-in method and the dates you created the account and last signed in)
Daily challenge results: your first name and last initial (for example "Gaurav K."), or "Hidden player" if you turn off Profile → Show my name on leaderboards, plus your result: time, mistakes and hints (and, for Number recall and Math, how many digits or questions you got) To rank you on the daily leaderboards, which anyone using TezMind can see (including players who aren't signed in) Google Cloud Firestore (one result per daily challenge you play, up to four a day)
Game history: games played, level, score, time taken, and your theme and sound settings To show your history, progress, streaks and best scores Only on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers.
Advertising data: device advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions To show optional rewarded video ads (for extra lives and hints) and measure them Collected by Google AdMob under Google's Privacy Policy

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, files or payment information, and we do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In and Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore), provided by Google LLC: Firebase privacy
  • Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity, provided by Google LLC, which checks that requests come from the genuine app on a genuine device (to stop cheating and abuse): Firebase privacy
  • Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC: how Google uses information from apps that use its services

Ads and your consent

Ads in TezMind are optional: you choose to watch one for a reward. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Profile → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

How long we keep information

Your account information is kept until you delete your account. Game history stays on your device until you delete your account, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, open Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your Firebase account, your users record and your daily leaderboard results, and clears the game history on that device. If you no longer have the app, follow the steps on the account deletion page.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database security rules allow each signed-in user to read and write only their own record. Leaderboard results (display name and results only: time, mistakes, hints and score) can be read by anyone using the app, and each player can post only their own result, once per day.

Children

TezMind is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
